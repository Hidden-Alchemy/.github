## The Alchemy Process

Every repository in this organization occupies a stage of the same pipeline:

| Stage | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **IDEA** | A raw idea is written down and submitted, nothing more. |
| **CONCEPT** | The idea is examined for actual value and boundary — what it is, what it is not. |
| **ARCHITECTURE** | The shape of the eventual system is decided before code is written. |
| **SYSTEM** | A working thing exists: built, tested, and honest about its limits. |
| **AUTOMATION** | The repetitive parts get automated so the system creates leverage. |
| **REALITY** | The system is used, maintained, and — when its time passes — archived with a reason. |

Most ideas do not fail because they are bad. They fail somewhere between concept and execution. Hidden Alchemy exists to close that gap — publicly, so the process itself can be inspected and shared.

- **Research & Experimentation** — genuine investigation and small, labeled experiments that may or may not become products; failure is a recorded outcome, not a secret.

## Experimental Lab

Experiments here are short-lived, honestly-labeled explorations that have not yet earned a dedicated repository: a hypothesis, an objective, a status, and a next step — and, when appropriate, a recorded dead end.

- Concepts and raw ideas are submitted through the **Idea Submission** form (the `ideas` repository, being stood up as part of the org foundation work).
- Short-lived experimental code lives in the `experiments` repository — created only when the first real experiment exists, not before.
- Every experiment declares its lifecycle status; not every experiment becomes a product, and that is intentional.

## How to Participate

Anyone can contribute publicly without any organizational membership. The pathway:

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Hidden-Alchemy/.github/main/assets/svg/contribution-pathway.svg" width="100%" alt="EXPLORE → DISCUSS → CONTRIBUTE → COLLABORATE → RECOGNITION → TRUST → MEMBERSHIP ELIGIBILITY" />

The entry points are one of three issue forms, or a direct pull request to documentation:

- **Found a bug?** — [Bug Report form](https://github.com/Hidden-Alchemy/.github/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)
- **Have an idea for the lab?** — [Idea Submission form](https://github.com/Hidden-Alchemy/ideas/issues/new?template=idea_submission.yml) (lives in the `ideas` repo)
- **Proposing a concrete project?** — [Project Proposal form](https://github.com/Hidden-Alchemy/ideas/issues/new?template=project_proposal.yml)
- **Documentation fix?** — open a pull request directly against the relevant repository; no pre-approval needed for small fixes.

Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/Hidden-Alchemy/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) before your first contribution, and review the [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/Hidden-Alchemy/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) — both are short and worth the two minutes.

## Organization Principles

| | Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Build, Don't Perform** | What works matters more than what looks impressive in a demo. |
| 02 | **Complexity Should Become Invisible** | Good engineering hides unnecessary complexity from the user. |
| 03 | **Automation Should Create Leverage** | Automate what should not require human repetition — not everything. |
| 04 | **Design Is Part of Engineering** | A technically correct system can still be a bad product. |
| 05 | **Experiment Relentlessly** | Prototype. Break. Measure. Refine. Repeat — and record the outcome honestly. |
| 06 | **Details Compound** | Small decisions become large differences when repeated across a system. |

## Join the Lab

> GitHub organization membership is not the same as contributing to Hidden Alchemy. Anyone can contribute publicly without ever becoming an organization member. Membership is a separate, reviewed status granted to people who have already demonstrated sustained, trustworthy contribution.

**Membership is earned through contribution, not requested on demand.**

The bar, plainly:

- Be a **Recognized Contributor**: at least three accepted contributions spanning at least a month.
- Submit the Membership Interest form (in the `community` repo, live as part of the org foundation work).
- A human maintainer reviews the request against explicit, published criteria and the decision is recorded on the issue. Organization invitations are always sent manually — never automated.

See [GOVERNANCE.md](https://github.com/Hidden-Alchemy/.github/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md) for the full model.
