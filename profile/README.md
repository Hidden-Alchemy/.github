<div style="max-width:760px;margin:0 auto 24px"><b style="font-size:16px;color:#212B36">Field notes — what people usually ask</b></div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;font-size:15px;font-weight:700">How do I know what stage a project is in?</summary>
<div style="font-size:15px;color:#57606A;line-height:1.8;margin-top:10px">Every repository carries its current pipeline stage and a lifecycle status tag (ACTIVE, CONCEPT, EXPERIMENT, ARCHIVED) in its README — and the org-wide <code style="font-family:SFMono-Regular,Consolas,Menlo,monospace">repo-health-check</code> verifies those stay honest. The live <b>Org Pulse</b> snapshot at the top of this page repaints the same story every day.</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;font-size:15px;font-weight:700">What counts as a contribution?</summary>
<div style="font-size:15px;color:#57606A;line-height:1.8;margin-top:10px">A merged pull request, or an idea that is promoted into a real repository. At least three accepted contributions spanning at least a month earn the <b>Recognized Contributor</b> tier — the bar for the Membership Interest form.</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;font-size:15px;font-weight:700">Can I join as a member on day one?</summary>
<div style="font-size:15px;color:#57606A;line-height:1.8;margin-top:10px">No — membership is earned through contribution, not requested on demand. But it is never required either: you can contribute publicly without any membership at all, at every tier above the membership line.</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;font-size:15px;font-weight:700">Does automation ever approve anything?</summary>
<div style="font-size:15px;color:#57606A;line-height:1.8;margin-top:10px">Automation only assists — labelling, welcome messages, stale reminders. Decisions that change a project's stage or a person's membership are always made and recorded by a human.</div>
</details>
